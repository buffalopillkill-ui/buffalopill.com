SECTION 01
ORIGIN & EARLY LIFE

Subject was born in Nevada, later relocating to Pillkill, NV — a township that does not appear on any state-issued map and is listed in no census. Its existence is confirmed only by streaming service metadata and a single gas station receipt recovered from a murder scene.

At an undetermined point in his adolescence, subject became fixated on actor Matthew Lillard, consuming his filmography on a compulsive loop. This obsession escalated following repeated viewings of the 1996 film Scream. Subject legally attempted to change his name to MATTHEW THE FRICK LILLARD — request was denied by the Clark County clerk on grounds of "disturbing intent."

The obsession did not stop at cinema. Subject began to identify with the film's primary antagonist, eventually moving from identification to imitation.

SECTION 02
KNOWN CRIMINAL HISTORY

Subject's first confirmed victim was his own mother. Details of the incident are too graphic for standard filing. Following this event, subject systematically eliminated the remainder of his bloodline — his grandmother, his sister — before expanding his activity beyond family units.

Subject has been shot a confirmed multiple times across various incidents. He has survived every instance. PKPD ballistics team has no explanation for this. Dr. ████ ████████ of the Nevada State Medical Board described his continued survival as "physiologically impossible and deeply upsetting."

The name "Buffalo Pill" was assigned to subject by a victim during an active incident who reportedly said, "What are you? Some sort of buffalo pill?" Subject paused, replied "Huh. Buffalo Pill. I think I like that name," and completed the encounter. PKPD considers this the official origin of the alias.

Most notable incident: Las Vegas, NV — committed in collaboration with associate known as MK ULTRA (see Section 03). Exact nature of the Las Vegas incident remains classified above this clearance level. What we can confirm: it was bad.

SECTION 05
PSYCHOLOGICAL PROFILE

Subject presents as perpetually angry with documented episodes of emotional vulnerability. He is afraid of two things: losing, and being made fun of.

Subject has been married twice. Both marriages ended under circumstances PKPD has declined to document. He owns a dog named Scooby Doo, whom he loves unconditionally. Agents report his demeanor in the presence of the dog is "completely different. Like a different guy entirely. It's weird."

Subject also maintains a deep personal attachment to his knife. Forensics have declined to run further tests on it.
